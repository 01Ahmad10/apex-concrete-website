HOW A FLOOR COMES TOGETHER

Care at every stage.

A clear sequence helps you understand the work ahead. Assessment, preparation, finishing and care each play a part in the result.

01

Site assessment & quote

Start with your floor area, location, photos and intended use. A slab assessment helps identify existing coatings, repairs and access requirements, so the proposed scope and finish can be discussed before work starts.

02

Grinding & preparation

Prepare the concrete for the selected system. This may involve removing old surface material and working through grinding stages, with attention to the floor’s condition and the finish being sought.

03

Finishing & application

Follow the agreed polishing or sealing approach. The sequence is matched to the system and slab, with attention to the main floor area, edges and transitions.

04

Walkthrough & aftercare

Review the completed surface and discuss appropriate cleaning, maintenance and any return-to-use requirements. Knowing how to care for the selected system is part of a considered handover.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before we begin.

Can an existing concrete floor be polished?

Often, but the condition of the slab matters. Existing coatings, cracks, repairs and variations can influence the result. Send photos so we can discuss assessment and preparation.

How do I choose between polishing and grind & seal?

The systems create their finish in different ways. We’ll discuss the appearance you want, the use of the space and ongoing care before recommending an approach.

What affects the cost and timeframe?

Floor area, slab condition, access, coating removal and the selected finish all affect the scope. A project-specific quote is the best way to establish pricing and timing.

What should I send with my enquiry?

Your suburb, approximate floor area, photos or plans, whether it’s a new or existing slab, and your preferred timing. You can email these directly to Apex.
